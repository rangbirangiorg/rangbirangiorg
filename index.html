why we made this

A lot of us grew up thinking we had to pick one: be queer, or be South Asian. Like the two didn't fit in the same body. We don't think that's true, so we built a place where they don't have to compete.

That's really it. A spot on the internet for South Asian LGBTQ+ people to find each other, read stories that sound like their own, and get actual help when they need it, no matter where in the world they've ended up.

join us

this bit's easy, fill out the form below and we'll take it from there.

Where two worlds meet, and both are whole:

Rang Birangi (it means "colorful" in Hindi and Urdu) exists because too many of us grew up believing we had to choose: our queerness, or our culture. We refuse that false binary.

We preserve LGBTQ+ South Asian stories that might otherwise disappear, break cultural stigma through honest storytelling, and build a community where every identity is celebrated, not despite heritage, but within it.
